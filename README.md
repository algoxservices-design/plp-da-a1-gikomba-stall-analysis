## Business Problem

Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market, where she manually records her daily sales and maintains basic inventory records. She needs to understand how her business is performing, whether her revenue is growing, which days generate the most sales, and which products require restocking. This project uses core Python to analyze 14 days of sales data and product inventory, calculate business performance indicators, identify sales patterns, evaluate gross profit margins, and provide data-driven recommendations to help Wanjiku make better business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Business Growth:** Total revenue over the 14-day period was **KES 46,450**. Weekly revenue increased from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2, representing **10.66% growth**.

2. **Weekend Sales Performance:** Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, compared to weekday average sales of KES 2,685. This represents an **82.50% increase** in weekend sales.

3. **Inventory and Profitability:** Tomatoes (18 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg) were below their respective reorder levels. Avocado recorded the highest gross margin at **42.86%**, followed by Sukuma at 40%.
